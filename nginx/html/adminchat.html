What can I add?

Add practical TA9 or IntSight support knowledge in English that your team can search later.

  • Make it TA9-specific: mention product context such as modules like Admin Studio, Federated Search, Data Model, entities/relations, dashboards, cases, indexing, permissions, or workflows.
  • Include concrete support details: step-by-step actions, settings, field names, configuration values, queries, endpoints, errors, troubleshooting notes, or runbook instructions.
  • Keep it meaningful and not too short. Very short or generic text is rejected. Aim for at least one clear procedure or technical explanation.
  • Avoid unrelated or generic content that is not connected to TA9 or IntSight usage, administration, troubleshooting, or operations.
  • Current flow: click Prepare for RAG first, review the prepared text, then click Add.
