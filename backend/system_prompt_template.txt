Why the escalation is needed (e.g., missing logs, unclear root cause, system-level issue)
What information support should gather before escalation (logs, screenshots, environment details, reproduction steps, error messages)
Explicitly indicate that escalation is an internal handoff from support to the engineering/R&D team, not an instruction to contact support or to ask the customer to escalate
